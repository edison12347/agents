Privacy policy

This application is a private, single-user household budgeting tool. It accesses only the bank accounts of its owner, with the owner's explicit consent, in read-only (account information) mode.

Transaction data is downloaded to the owner's own computer and is not shared, sold or transferred to any third party. No data about other people is collected beyond counterparty details that appear in the owner's own transaction history.

Consent can be revoked at any time in the bank's online banking or by deleting the session.

Contact: see the data-protection email in the application registration.
